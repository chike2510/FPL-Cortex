Questions, answered

Start with the facts.

A straightforward product deserves a straightforward connection flow.

Do I need a separate Cortex account?

No. The app is designed around your FPL account. There is no Cortex signup or Cortex password to remember.

Can I use Cortex before Gameweek 1?

Yes. The public player pool and the pre-season squad builder do not depend on a live current gameweek.

What happens when I connect?

Cortex asks the server to authenticate with FPL, resolves your entry from the authenticated account, and then loads the official team state. If FPL rejects the request, the app shows that error instead of pretending the Team ID was a login.
